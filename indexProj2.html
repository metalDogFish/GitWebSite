Project Slime Mold

Slime Mold growth with emergant behaviors

4000 individual slime Molds that steer towards the light (each other).
Using pixel detection at a limited range, slime molds can produce a variety of different behaviors and visual patterns by adjusting the angles of their sensors
Each slime mold has 3 sensors, 1 in front and 2 angled off to each side by degrees of separation like this peace symbol below
Mold will turn towards the angle sensor that detects the highest amount of white light.
If no sensors detect any light, mold continues moving straight forward

This project uses javascript and the P5 library

Slime Molds- changing the sensor angle results in wild variations..
