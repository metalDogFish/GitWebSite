Hello, and welcome to my portfolio!

In my youth, I experienced the golden age of video-game arcades when a handful of quarters became hours of fun and excitment. The huge impact these games had when they were first introduced, classics such as; Donkey Kong, Pac Man, Dragons Lair and Asteroids. One time, our school made news headlines for creating pandamonium in the mall, because hundreds of us showed up to compete in an Atari Asteroids Competition Event sponsered by Pepsi and our local arcade.

It blows me away how far digital gaming has evolved in my lifetime, and how affordable and powerful the tools to create have become. The processes and mathmatics of digital motion and creating animations have always fascinated me. Here, I present a portfolio collection of coding projects including several demos and games using Unity and P5.js!
