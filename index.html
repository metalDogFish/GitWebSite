Hello, and welcome to my portfolio!

I experienced the golden age of video-game arcades when a handful of quarters became hours of fun and excitment. The huge impact these games had when they were first introduced, classics such as; Donkey Kong, Pac Man, Dragons Lair and Asteroids. Our whole school took over the local mall creating pandamonium one weekend, because the local arcade hosted an Atari Asteroids competition sponsered by Pepsi, once only.

It blows me away how far digital gaming has evolved in my lifetime, and how affordable and efficient the tools to make them have become. I've created several games using flash, Unity and Unreal game engines, as well as native Android apps and demos. Digital design has always fascinated me, so my portfolio here may reflect a combination of those intrests.
