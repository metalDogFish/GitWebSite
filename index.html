Hello, and welcome to my portfolio!

Growing up during the golden age of arcade gaming, I experienced firsthand how a handful of quarters could turn into hours of excitement. Classic titles like Donkey Kong, Pac-Man, Dragon's Lair, and Asteroids left a lasting impression on me. At one point, my school even made local headlines when hundreds of students flooded a mall to compete in an Atari Asteroids tournament sponsored by Pepsi and a local arcade. Seeing how far games have evolved since then continues to inspire me. Today’s tools make it possible to create rich, interactive experiences in ways that once seemed unimaginable. I’ve always been fascinated by the systems behind games—especially the mathematics of motion, interaction, and animation. This portfolio showcases a collection of my work, including coding projects, interactive demos, and games built using Unity and p5.js. Each project reflects my passion for blending technical problem-solving with engaging gameplay design.
