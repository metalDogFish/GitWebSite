Hello, and welcome to my portfolio!

I'm old enough to remember how exciting text-based games were as a kid growing up in a small town. "You look East, and see a door with smoke spilling out from the darkness within"

I experienced the golden age of video-game arcades when a handful of quarters became hours of fun and excitment. Our whole school took over the local mall one weekend because the arcade hosted an Atari Asteroids competition once.

It blows me away how far digital gaming has evolved in my lifetime, and how affordable and efficient the tools to make them have become. I've created several games using flash, Unity and Unreal game engines, as well as native Android apps and demos. Digital design has always fascinated me, so my portfolio here may reflect a combination of those intrests.
